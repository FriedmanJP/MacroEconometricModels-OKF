# Panel

Panel-data estimators: dynamic multivariate panels, single-equation regression and discrete choice, treatment-effect designs, and distributed-lag cointegration.

* [Panel VAR (PVAR)](/panel/pvar.md) - GMM and fixed-effects estimation of panel VARs with IRFs, FEVD, stability diagnostics, and bootstrap inference.
* [Panel Regression (xtreg/xtivreg/xtlogit/xtprobit)](/panel/preg.md) - Linear, IV, and discrete-choice panel estimators with HDFE absorption, specification tests, and robust covariances.
* [Difference-in-Differences and Event Study LP](/panel/did.md) - Staggered-adoption DiD with heterogeneity-robust estimators, Bacon/negative-weight diagnostics, HonestDiD sensitivity, and LP event-study estimators.
* [ARDL, NARDL, and Panel ARDL](/panel/ardl.md) - OLS ARDL estimation with long-run multipliers, PSS bounds testing, asymmetric NARDL, and PMG/MG/DFE panel estimators.
