# Univariate

Single-series time-series models: ARIMA-class estimation, conditional
volatility, latent stochastic volatility, trend-cycle filters, seasonal
adjustment, and spectral analysis.

* [ARIMA Models](/univariate/arima.md) - Univariate AR, MA, ARMA, ARIMA, SARIMA, and ARFIMA estimation with order selection and forecasting.
* [ARCH Models](/univariate/arch.md) - Engle ARCH(q) models for time-varying conditional variance with MLE estimation and variance forecasting.
* [GARCH Models](/univariate/garch.md) - GARCH, EGARCH, GJR-GARCH, GARCH-MIDAS, FIGARCH, IGARCH, component GARCH, and APARCH volatility models.
* [Stochastic Volatility](/univariate/sv.md) - Bayesian stochastic volatility models with latent AR(1) log-variance estimated by Gibbs sampling.
* [Time Series Filters](/univariate/filters.md) - HP, Hamilton, Beveridge-Nelson, Baxter-King, and boosted HP trend-cycle decompositions.
* [X-13ARIMA-SEATS Seasonal Adjustment](/univariate/x13.md) - Pure-Julia X-13ARIMA-SEATS seasonal adjustment with X-11 and SEATS decompositions and automatic ARIMA identification.
* [Spectral Analysis](/univariate/spectral.md) - ACF/PACF, spectral density, cross-spectrum, frequency-domain filtering, and white-noise diagnostics.
